Privacy Policy for Mood Mirror

Last Updated: October 25, 2025

Your privacy is critically important to us. At Mood Mirror, we have a few fundamental principles:

Your Photos Stay Yours

All photo processing, meme creation, and emoji placement happens directly in your browser on your device. Your images are never uploaded to our servers.

Information We Collect

  • Non-Personal Information: Standard web logs (browser type, language, referring site, timestamps) may be collected to understand usage.
  • Cookies: Used mainly to remember your ad consent preferences. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Advertising and Third Parties

We use Google AdSense to support the site. Depending on your region and choices, Google may use cookies or local storage to deliver and measure ads. In the EEA/UK/CH, our consent banner lets you accept, reject, or customize ad preferences. If you reject tracking, you may still see non‑personalized ads.

Learn more: Google Ads policies · How Google uses information

Your Choices

  • Use the consent banner (where shown) to manage ad personalization.
  • Click anytime to reopen the banner.
  • Manage or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We’ll update the “Last Updated” date above when we do.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us or email moodmirror.app@gmail.com.
